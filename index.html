About Tirtha Electrical & Services

Professional Electrical Solutions You Can Rely On

Tirtha Electrical & Services is a professional electrical contracting and services company based in Jalgaon, Maharashtra. We provide reliable electrical solutions for projects of different scales, with a focus on quality workmanship, safety, timely execution and client satisfaction.
